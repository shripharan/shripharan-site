Tips for better results
Clean lines work best
Sketches with clear, confident linework produce better results than heavily textured or shaded drawings. Use pen on white paper or a clean digital sketch.
Be specific in your prompt
Include material descriptions, lighting conditions, time of day, and style references. "Photorealistic" or "architectural render" in the prompt helps steer the output.
Guidance scale
Low (1–4): AI interprets freely, looser to sketch. Medium (5–10): balanced. High (10+): tightly follows sketch geometry but can reduce quality.
